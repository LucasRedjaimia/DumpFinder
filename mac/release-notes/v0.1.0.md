First preview of Dump Finder for Mac (Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later). Read-only: it changes nothing on your Mac.

## What's new
- Kernel panics: reads the panic reports, explains the common causes (system hang, sleep / wake, GPU, SSD, USB / Thunderbolt…) and names the non-Apple extensions involved.
- Unexpected restarts: reads how the previous session ended (watchdog, forced restart with the power button).
- App crashes and low-memory events over the last 30 days.
- Third-party kernel extensions (loaded or left behind) and system extensions.
- Startup disk free space, SSD health (SMART), battery health, SIP and Gatekeeper.
- Copy the report to the clipboard (⇧⌘C), scan again (⌘R).
