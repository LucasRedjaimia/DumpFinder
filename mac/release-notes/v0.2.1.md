A fix release for Dump Finder for Mac 0.2.0.

## Fixes
- The window can be moved again: drag its title bar (outside the buttons). A double-click on the title bar zooms the window, as on any Mac app.

## Install
1. Unzip and move **Dump Finder.app** to Applications (open at login and the MCP server need it there).
2. The app is not notarized yet, so macOS blocks it the first time: open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. Only once. (On macOS 14, right-click the app › Open also works.)

Already on 0.2.0? Dump Finder offers this version at launch; your settings, history and change journal are kept.
