## What's new
- **Optimize › Storage**: see what takes space on your Mac and free it safely.
  - **Apps**: every app with its size and the last time you opened it.
  - **Folders**: your home folder sorted by size, to drill into folder by folder.
  - **Largest files**: the 60 biggest files, wherever they are (virtual machine disks are counted by the space they really use).
  - Each item has **Show in Finder** and **Move to Trash**. A move to the Trash can be undone from Tools › History as long as the Trash is not emptied.
  - Essentials are protected (Documents, Desktop, Library data, keychains, mail, iCloud Drive, SSH keys and shell settings…): only Show in Finder is offered for them.
  - Apps installed by an administrator go to the Trash through the Finder, which asks for your password.

The first analysis may make macOS ask to let Dump Finder read some folders (Desktop, Documents, Downloads, data from other apps): allow it so they are measured. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Install
1. Unzip and move **Dump Finder.app** to Applications.
2. The app is not notarized yet, so macOS blocks it the first time: open **System Settings › Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**. Only once. (On macOS 14, right-click the app › Open also works.)

Already on 0.2.x? Dump Finder offers this version at launch; your settings, history and change journal are kept.
