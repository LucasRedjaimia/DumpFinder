## What's new
- **Optimize › Storage**: see what takes the most space on your PC, then free it up.
  - Look in your user folder or a whole drive (C:, D:…).
  - **Programs**: installed programs, largest first, with **Uninstall** (the program's own uninstaller).
  - **Folders**: drill down from the largest folder to the largest subfolder.
  - **Largest files**: the 60 biggest files, wherever they are.
  - Every row has **Show in Explorer**, and files and folders can be **moved to the Recycle Bin**. That can be undone from **Tools › History** (or the Recycle Bin) as long as the Recycle Bin is not emptied. If Windows cannot keep an item in the Recycle Bin, Windows warns you before deleting it.
  - The essentials are protected and only get Show in Explorer: Windows, Program Files, other accounts, system files, your standard folders (Documents, Desktop, Downloads…), AppData's Windows and Store app data, and your keys (.ssh…).
  - OneDrive files that only live in the cloud count for nothing, since they take no space on the PC.
