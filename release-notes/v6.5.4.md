## What's new
- **Drivers: real update check**: the Drivers page (Hardware › Drivers) now asks Windows Update which newer drivers exist for your hardware, instead of judging drivers only by their age.
  - A driver with a newer version on Windows Update gets an orange "Update available · version" badge, and its Update button opens Windows Update's optional updates, where that driver is offered.
  - An old driver with nothing newer on Windows Update (Steam Streaming, Xbox Wireless Adapter…) is now shown in neutral grey, "9 years · nothing newer", instead of an orange warning. An old driver is not a problem by itself as long as it does not cause crashes.
  - A line above the list shows the result and when it was checked, with a "Check again" button. The search only reads the catalog: nothing is downloaded or installed. Its answer is kept for 12 hours.
  - If Windows Update cannot be reached, the badges fall back to the driver's age, as before.

## Fixes
- Gigabyte motherboards: when Gigabyte Update keeps coming back after being uninstalled, the "Third-party drivers & OEM services" card now explains why. The motherboard BIOS copies the service back into Windows at every boot. The guide shows where to turn this off in the BIOS (Settings › Miscellaneous), then how to stop the copy already installed.
